Problem 1 — Municipal water pumpEasy

Given: Q = 60 m³/h, ρ = 1000 kg/m³, H = 25 m, η = 78%

Step 1 — Hydraulic power: P_h = ρgQH / 3,600,000 = (1000 × 9.81 × 60 × 25) / 3,600,000 = 4.088 kW

Step 2 — Brake power: P_b = P_h / η = 4.088 / 0.78 = 5.240 kW

Answer: Hydraulic 4.09 kW, brake power 5.24 kW
