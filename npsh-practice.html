Problem 1 — Cold water, flooded suctionEasy

Given: Pa = 101.325 kPa, Pv = 2.34 kPa, ρ = 998 kg/m³, Hz = 4 m, Hf = 0.6 m, NPSHr = 3.5 m

Step 1 — Pressure margin term: (101.325 − 2.34) × 1000 / (998 × 9.81) = 10.11 m

Step 2 — NPSHa: 10.11 + (4) − 0.6 = 13.51 m

Step 3 — Compare with NPSHr: 13.51 m vs 3.5 m → safe with a comfortable margin

Answer: NPSHa ≈ 13.51 m (safe with a comfortable margin)
