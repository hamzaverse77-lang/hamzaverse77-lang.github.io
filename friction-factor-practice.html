Problem 1 — Water in PVC pipeEasy

Given: ρ = 998 kg/m³, μ = 0.001 Pa·s, v = 1.5 m/s, D = 0.08 m, L = 30 m, ε = 0.0015 mm

Step 1 — Reynolds number: Re = (998 × 1.5 × 0.08) / 0.001 = 119,760

Step 2 — Friction factor (turbulent, Swamee-Jain): ε/D = 0.00002; f = 0.01734

Step 3 — Head loss: h_f = f(L/D)(v²/2g) = 0.01734 × (30/0.08) × 0.1147 = 0.746 m

Answer: h_f ≈ 0.746 m
