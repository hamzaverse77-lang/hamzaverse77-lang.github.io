Problem 1 — Water cooler, direct LMTDEasy

Given: Q = 400 kW, U = 900 W/m²·K, LMTD = 35 °C

Step 1 — Formula: A = Q / (U × LMTD)

Step 2 — Substitute (Q in W): A = 400,000 / (900 × 35)

Step 3 — Result: A = 12.70 m²

Answer: A ≈ 12.70 m²

Problem 4 — From temperatures, counter-currentMedium

Given: counter-current, hot 130→85 °C, cold 20→70 °C, Q = 350 kW, U = 700 W/m²·K

Step 1 — ΔT₁, ΔT₂: 60 °C, 65 °C

Step 2 — LMTD: (60 − 65) / ln(60/65) = 62.47 °C

Step 3 — Formula: A = Q / (U × LMTD)

Step 4 — Substitute (Q in W): A = 350,000 / (700 × 62.47)

Step 5 — Result: A = 8.00 m²

Answer: A ≈ 8.00 m²
